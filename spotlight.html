before you send anything

This shop isn’t real.
The page it’s built on is.

Between the Veil is a concept — a demonstration of what an author feature can look like when someone actually designs it. Wren, Idris and Tova are invented. There’s no submission queue, because there’s no shop.

What’s real is that pages like this get built, for authors and for bookish small businesses, by Sagewave. If you’ve got a book and nowhere to send people, that’s the conversation.
